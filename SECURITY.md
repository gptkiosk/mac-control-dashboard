# Security policy

## Private vulnerability reporting

Do not post vulnerability details, credentials, private logs or sensitive fixes in public issues or pull requests.

Private vulnerability reporting is enabled for [gptkiosk/mac-control-dashboard](https://github.com/gptkiosk/mac-control-dashboard/security/advisories/new). Use that repository’s private reporting form. This does not promise ongoing maintenance, a response or a fix.

To report privately:

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
2. Confirm the report is private before entering sensitive details.
3. Describe the affected version, component, reproduction steps and impact. Sanitize logs and omit credentials and personal identifiers.

If the private reporting option is absent, do not substitute a public issue or pull request. Ask the maintainer to establish a private reporting channel first, without including vulnerability details.

## Review and disclosure

MCD is distributed as-is with no ongoing upstream maintenance or security updates promised. A private reporting channel, when verified, does not guarantee a response or a fix. Operators and fork owners are responsible for evaluating their deployments and maintaining their own versions. Security review, credential rotation and publication remain explicit operator decisions.

## Forks

A fork owner should establish and verify a separate private reporting channel and state their own support policy. Do not assume the upstream reporting form covers a fork’s changes or users.
